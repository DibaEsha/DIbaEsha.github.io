My Brief Bio

From a childhood fascination with atlases to a career in Business Intelligence, my journey has always been about understanding the world through data. With over eight years of experience in financial analysis and MIS, I am now merging my business acumen with technical geospatial skills through a BTech in GIS at BCIT.

My expertise spans Python, SQL, and photogrammetry, with a focus on applying spatial solutions to the forestry, mining, energy, and municipal sectors. When I’m not analyzing data, you can find me hiking or off-roading, exploring the very landscapes I map.
